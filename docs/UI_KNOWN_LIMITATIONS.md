| L16 | **Admin screens measured wider than the phone viewport with no horizontal scroll.** | **Resolved** — root cause fixed, width regression gated | **Root cause:** `.shell__body` declared only `grid-template-rows`, so its single column was an **implicit `auto` track sized from the content's min-content width**. A five-tab strip, or a stacked table cell whose value could not shrink, pushed that track — and `main` — past the shell, and the shell's own `overflow: hidden` clipped the excess. That is why `documentElement.scrollWidth` always equalled the viewport (page overflow 0) while `main` measured **384 px at 320** and **409 px at 390**. **Structural fix:** the column is now `minmax(0, 1fr)`; the tab strip wraps instead of scrolling under a hidden scrollbar (`.tabs` had `overflow-x: auto; scrollbar-width: none`); stacked table cells wrap, their label can shrink (`flex: 0 1 auto; max-width: 45%`) and their controls carry `min-width: 0`. **Measured** over 10 admin/reviewer routes × six viewports: **21/60 combinations exceeded the viewport before and 0/60 after** (clinic **384→320**, privacy **409→390**, audit **365→320**, retention **364→320**, profile **341→320** at 320 widths; the 320×400 row repeats the same values), and every `main` right edge now equals the viewport with no visible element past it. **Regression gate** `e2e/layout-width.spec.js` — **31 tests**: 24 session×viewport measurements (admin, reviewer, patient, caregiver over all six sizes) asserting document width, `main` width, `main` right edge, `main` scroll width, no child past the edge, no tab hidden inside a strip and no stacked row wider than its panel, plus the tablet bounded-panel contract, the confirmation dialog at three phone sizes, and the click-reached patient/episode detail screens at three phone sizes. |

## Things that are *not* limitations

- The landing page scrolls. That is the one screen allowed to scroll, and it is
  intentional.
- The 3D canvas does not accept pointer input. That is required, not missing.
- There is no loading spinner over the hero while the scene downloads. The static
  gradient is the loading state by design.
- A context loss, missing WebGL or a scene error shows no error text. That is the
  required behaviour for a decorative element.
- The compact metric bars are `aria-hidden`: the same numbers are in an
  accessible table beside them.
